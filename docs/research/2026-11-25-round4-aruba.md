## Public customer-advisory source found

HPE publishes a public **Customer Advisory**, [a00142509en_us](https://support.hpe.com/hpesc/public/docDisplay?docId=a00142509en_us&docLocale=en_US), about qualified 100Gb cables producing unsupported-transceiver messages. Its revision history gives 26 August 2024 as original publication and 8 May 2025 as revision. These are notice dates, not software release dates. The scope explicitly identifies CX 8325/9300 and particular cables with any switch OS version. Resolution says functionality is unaffected. It is an **out-of-cohort exclusion control**, not a 6200/6300 notice, upgrade requirement or withdrawal. This establishes a public customer-advisory route but does not close the gap in a complete, comparable 6200/6300 non-security archive.

The packet also retains [HPESB3P04641](https://support.hpe.com/hpesc/public/docDisplay?docId=emr_na-hpesb3p04641en_us) as a separate security context example. It explicitly names both study series and 10.13 affected/fixed versions. The embedded publication date, portal release date and history initial-publication date differ; all remain separate. It is not counted as a non-security field notice or evidence of withdrawal.
