# Reviewing the comparison plans

The security-gateway, switching and management plans are evidence review queues.
Their common 24-CVE dependency cohort is purposive and cannot estimate population
vulnerability rates. Every selected family receives the same CVEs.

The public report includes the question, unit, endpoint, selection rule, denominator
limits, date basis, unknown policy and confounders. Cell evidence includes source
fingerprints, capture/publisher dates, product labels, platforms, version scope and
extraction locators. Examples are capped at twenty statements, with truncation
declared. Undated and outside-period evidence is visible as excluded. Fixed-only
and investigating-only statements are unresolved, not proof of prior exposure.

## Adjudication sequence

1. Follow the exact source and check its fingerprint. A revised live document is
   not automatically the old retained evidence.
2. Establish the product role, platforms, software branches and enabled features.
   Split mixed ranges only with explicit publisher evidence. An upstream version
   match does not settle appliance impact.
3. Review CVE-specific applicability and exceptions. Keep missing, shared, undated,
   unresolved and access-blocked evidence distinct.
4. Establish supported-model/release-train inventories and equivalent deployment
   configurations for every comparison member. Family labels alone are insufficient.
5. Design a separate outcome-independent cohort for the precise research question.
   Define upstream components, CVE inclusion/exclusion criteria, fixed observation
   window, authoritative enumeration sources and handling of rejected/changed CVEs
   before inspecting vendor outcomes. Freeze and fingerprint the resulting list.
6. Obtain independent human adjudication, resolve disagreements against retained
   primary sources, and report missingness and access differences alongside results.

Do not divide affected-CVE counts by imported advisories, product statements or
installed-base counts: their units differ. A fully populated reference table cannot
unlock population rates. Inventories, equivalence, appropriate cohort design and
independent review remain prerequisites.

Initial advisory dates group latest statements; they do not reconstruct as-of
exposure, first disclosure, firmware availability or patch delays. The prepared
independent-review packet and private worksheet remain a separate workflow.
