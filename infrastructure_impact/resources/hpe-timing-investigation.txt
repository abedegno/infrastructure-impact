Reviewed: 2026-10-25
Status: unresolved research candidate; no verified silent-fix or concealment finding.

Observed association
HPESBCR04733 identifies seven HPE Cray EX blade/server products, CcNc firmware
before 1.9.5-39, and HFP 24.8.1 dated 2024-08-28. Its Release Date field and
initial-history entry identify 2024-11-06. The reported date difference is 70 days.
The fix association and the earlier firmware date come from the same bulletin;
they are not independent evidence that customers could download it in August.
https://support.hpe.com/hpesc/public/api/document/hpesbcr04733en_us

level. Direct collection returned a page without a usable release table, and
browser inspection did not produce usable content. The raw response is retained
locally. An indexed excerpt is not being substituted for an archived release page

Questions that remain open

Assessment
The chronology is worth investigating. Firmware labels may be build dates or
vendor-reported release dates, and the earliest collected bulletin may be later
than a customer notification or another public notice. No intent is inferred.
The application keeps this at unverified_release_date_candidate and does not
register it as verified fix_evidence. Upgrading the finding requires archived
release-availability evidence plus the exact CVE/product/component association.
